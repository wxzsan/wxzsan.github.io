Quantum Query and Computational Complexity量子查询复杂度与计算复杂性

I enjoy the clean, elegant ideas that arise in quantum query complexity. In particular, I study algorithms and query lower bounds for simulation, ground-state preparation, and estimation. I also study the computational complexity of estimating spectral properties of Hamiltonians.我喜欢量子查询复杂度中涌现的简洁而优美的思想。具体而言，我研究量子模拟、基态制备和估计问题的算法与查询复杂度下界，也研究估计哈密顿量谱性质的计算复杂性。

Time-Dependent Hamiltonian Simulation with Optimal Query Complexity

Boyang Chen, Minbo Gao, Xinzhao Wang†, and Shuo Zhou.

AI useAI 使用说明

The authors initiated the project. LLMs generated the core ideas and proof strategies, which the authors refined and verified. Paper statement.项目由作者发起。核心思路与证明策略由大语言模型生成，再由作者完善并核验。论文原文说明。

We obtain optimal query complexity for Lipschitz-continuous time-dependent Hamiltonian simulation, matching the bound for time-independent Hamiltonians. We encode the full time-ordered evolution in a transducer and analyze catalyst-omission error using its private block. This analysis guides the choice of LCU coefficients for combining circuits with different transducer reuse counts, canceling leading error terms and yielding factorial error suppression.我们给出了 Lipschitz 连续含时哈密顿量模拟的最优查询复杂度，与不含时情形的界一致。我们将完整的时间有序演化编码进 transducer，并利用其 private block 分析省略催化态所引入的误差。该分析指引了不同 transducer 重用次数线路的 LCU 系数选择，通过消去主导误差项，实现阶乘级的误差衰减。

End-to-End Resource-Efficient Quantum Algorithms端到端资源高效的量子算法

Query complexity alone can leave substantial costs in oracle implementation unaccounted for. I am interested in designing quantum algorithms with end-to-end resource efficiency, from input access to circuit implementation. My work includes efficient implementations of common input oracles and algorithms for quantum simulation and QSVT that combine simple circuits with favorable asymptotic resource costs.仅考察查询复杂度，可能忽略实现预言机本身所需的大量资源。我希望从输入访问到线路实现，贯通量子算法的整个实现链条，设计端到端资源高效的算法。我的研究包括常用输入预言机的高效实现，以及兼具简洁线路与良好渐近资源复杂度的量子模拟和 QSVT 算法。

Product-Formula Methods and Error Mitigation乘积公式方法与误差缓解

For quantum simulation, product formulas use simple circuits and few ancillas, and their commutator-based error bounds exploit locality. I develop compensation and extrapolation methods for Hamiltonian and Lindbladian simulation that improve precision dependence while preserving these advantages, and study how to mitigate physical noise together with Trotter error.在量子模拟中，乘积公式的线路结构简单、辅助量子比特需求少，其基于对易子的误差界还能利用局域性。我发展用于哈密顿量与 Lindbladian 模拟的补偿和外推方法，在保持这些优势的同时改善精度依赖，并研究如何同时缓解物理噪声与 Trotter 误差。

Lindbladian Simulation with Commutator Bounds

Xinzhao Wang*, Shuo Zhou*, Xiaoyang Wang, Yi-Cong Zheng, Shengyu Zhang, and Tongyang Li.

We derive commutator-based Trotter error bounds for Lindbladian simulation, yielding \(O(\sqrt N)\) system-size scaling in the number of Trotter steps for local \(N\)-site systems. For observable estimation, Richardson extrapolation preserves this commutator scaling while making the number of steps per circuit polylogarithmic in inverse precision. To control the extrapolation remainder, we establish a finite-order BCH truncation bound in terms of doubly right-nested commutators, without assuming convergence of the full BCH series.我们建立了 Lindbladian 模拟中基于对易子的 Trotter 误差界，使局域 \(N\) 格点系统的 Trotter 步数具有 \(O(\sqrt N)\) 的系统规模依赖。在可观测量估计中，Richardson 外推保留了这一对易子标度，并使每条线路的步数仅以多对数方式依赖于精度的倒数。为控制外推余项，我们建立了基于双重右嵌套对易子的有限阶 BCH 截断界，而无须假设完整 BCH 级数收敛。

  • Trotter Error Compensation with Polylogarithmic Precision and Nested-Commutator Scaling without Ancillas

    Xinzhao Wang, Shuo Zhou, Ziruo Wang, Pei Zeng, Jinzhao Sun, Qi Zhao, Tom Gur, and Tongyang Li.

    For local Hamiltonians, high-order channel compensation improves the evolution-time and system-size scaling of a \(K\)-th-order product formula to that of order \(2K+1\), with polylogarithmic precision dependence per circuit and no ancillas.对于局域哈密顿量，高阶信道补偿使 \(K\) 阶乘积公式在演化时间和系统规模上的复杂度标度达到 \(2K+1\) 阶公式的水平；无须辅助量子比特，且每条线路对精度倒数仅有多对数依赖。

  • Joint Mitigation of Algorithmic and Physical Errors in Noisy Hamiltonian Simulation

    Shuo Zhou*, Xinzhao Wang*, Ruiqi Zhang*, Xiaoyang Wang, Yi-Cong Zheng, Tongyang Li, and Shengyu Zhang.

    Couples physical noise strength to the Trotter step size so that Richardson extrapolation cancels leading contributions from both Trotter error and physical noise along a one-dimensional path.将物理噪声强度与 Trotter 步长关联起来，通过一维 Richardson 外推同时消去 Trotter 误差和物理噪声的主导项。

  • Quantum Singular Value Transformation without Block Encodings

    Shantanav Chakraborty, Soumyabrata Hazra, Tongyang Li, Changpeng Shao, Xinzhao Wang†, and Yuxin Zhang.

    Implements QSVT with one ancilla and no multi-qubit controlled gates by approximating the Hamiltonian evolutions in generalized quantum signal processing with product formulas. Richardson extrapolation improves precision dependence while retaining near-optimal, commutator-sensitive gate complexity.用乘积公式近似广义量子信号处理中的哈密顿量演化，仅用一个辅助量子比特、无须多量子比特受控门即可实现 QSVT。Richardson 外推进一步改善精度依赖，并保留近最优且依赖于对易子的量子门复杂度。

Quantum Data Access and Circuit Implementation量子数据访问与线路实现

I use sparsity to reduce the T-gate cost of QROM, state preparation, and block encoding, and develop circuit implementations of Hamiltonian simulation and QSVT that use fewer gates and ancillas.我利用稀疏性降低 QROM、量子态制备和块编码的 \(T\) 门成本，并研究节省量子门与辅助量子比特的哈密顿量模拟和 QSVT 线路实现。

Optimal T Counts under Sparsity: from QROM to State Preparation and Block Encoding

Tongyang Li, Fengning Ou, Xinzhao Wang†, Penghui Yao, Pei Yuan, and Shengyu Zhang.

AI useAI 使用说明

In the second version, GPT-6 Astra helped develop approximate sparse QROM and extend the matching bounds to the near-dense regime. The authors verified and revised these arguments. Paper statement.第二版中，GPT-6 Astra 协助发展了近似稀疏 QROM 的构造，并将匹配界扩展至近稠密参数区间；相关论证由作者核验并修改。论文原文说明。

We prove matching \(T\)-count upper and lower bounds for sparse QROM, state preparation, and block encoding. Our upper bounds build on a multilevel hashing scheme for sparse QROM. The lower bounds hold even with adaptive measurements and classical control.我们证明了稀疏 QROM、量子态制备和块编码相匹配的 \(T\) 门数量上下界。上界构造基于稀疏 QROM 的多层哈希方案。即使允许自适应测量与经典控制，下界依然成立。

Online Learning and Equilibrium Computation在线学习与均衡计算

Matrix multiplicative weights connects online prediction with equilibrium computation. I study how its guarantees can adapt to the structure of a learning instance or to changing quantum states, and develop quantum algorithms for correlated equilibria in general-sum games.矩阵乘法权重更新将在线预测与均衡计算联系起来。我研究如何让学习保证适应具体实例的结构或随时间变化的量子态，并发展求解一般和博弈中相关均衡的量子算法。

Instance-Optimal Matrix Multiplicative Weight Update and Its Quantum Applications

Weiyuan Gong, Tongyang Li, Xinzhao Wang†, and Zhiyu Zhang.

We obtain instance-optimal regret for matrix learning from expert advice at the computational cost of standard matrix multiplicative weights. A one-sided Jensen trace inequality extends the potential-based analysis of matrix learning beyond the exponential potential used by standard MMWU.我们在保持标准矩阵乘法权重更新计算成本的同时，为矩阵专家建议学习给出了实例最优的遗憾界。单侧 Jensen 迹不等式将矩阵学习的势函数分析推广到标准 MMWU 所用的指数势函数之外。
